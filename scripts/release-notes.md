PaliCanon @VERSION@ for macOS on Apple silicon (M1 and later, macOS 13+).

**What is inside**: Learn (the alphabet and the words, your progress on this Mac, synced through your own Google Drive if you sign in), Search (the whole Pāli canon searched on your Mac by the yuga engine, no server), the Dictionary (the DPD, offline) and the Map.

**Why @SIZE_MIB@ MiB**: the download carries the search index and the language model that make Search and the dictionary work with no connection.

**Install**: open the DMG, drag PaliCanon to Applications. The app is signed and notarized; macOS opens it without a warning.

**Intel Macs** are not supported by this release. Windows and Linux: later.
